# Play Store listing — keyword research + ready-to-paste copy

Researched 2026-08-10. Google Play indexes **title, short description, and full
description** — there is no separate keyword field. Weighting, highest first:
title → short description → long description.

---

## The single biggest problem

Current title is **`GymCRM`** — 6 of 30 characters used.

The title is the highest-weighted field in Play Store search. Moving a target
keyword into the title reliably produces ranking movement within 48–72 hours.
You are currently spending 24 characters on nothing.

Both direct competitors already do this:

- `GymBook: Gym Management App`
- `My Gym : Gym Management App`

## READY TO PASTE

### Title (30 char limit)

```
GymCRM: Gym Management App
```

26 characters. Captures `gym management app`, `gym management`, `gym app`,
`management app`, and the brand.

**Alternate if you prefer "software":**
```
GymCRM: Gym Software & CRM
```
26 characters. Captures `gym software` and `gym CRM` but loses the
`gym management app` head phrase. Pick the first unless you have data saying
otherwise.

---

### Short description (80 char limit)

```
Gym management software: members, attendance, billing, QR check-in, dues
```

72 characters. Adds `gym management software`, `attendance`, `billing`,
`QR check-in`, and `dues` — none of which are in the title, so no wasted
overlap.

## Where else keywords need to go

**Screenshot captions.** Since 2025–26, text inside screenshots is read and
contributes to visibility, not just conversion. Put a short keyword caption
across the top of each screenshot:

1. "Member management & expiry tracking"
2. "QR code attendance — works offline"
3. "Fees, dues & PDF invoices"
4. "WhatsApp payment reminders"
5. "Member app with diet & workout plans"
6. "Reports, expenses & staff roles"

**Category:** Business (primary). Not Health & Fitness — that category is
dominated by consumer workout apps you cannot outrank and it attracts the wrong
installs.

**Tags:** pick the closest available to `business management`, `finance`,
`productivity`.

## The part nobody tells you

Google Play shifted from ranking on install volume to ranking on **retention and
engagement**. Keywords get you impressions; retention decides whether you keep
the ranking. Two consequences:

1. **Do not buy installs.** Low-quality installs that never open now actively
   damage ranking. This is why app install ads would hurt you.
2. **Watch Android Vitals.** A release that pushes ANR rate above 0.47%
   suppresses ranking within days, regardless of how good the keywords are.

**Reviews are the fastest lever you have.** You already ship
`review_prompt.dart`. Forty gyms with a working product should yield 25–30
reviews if you simply ask them on WhatsApp. Rating weight beats almost
everything else at your install volume.

---

## Suggested rollout

1. Change the title first, alone. Measure for 72 hours — title changes move
   fastest and you want a clean read.
2. Then short description.
3. Then full description + screenshot captions.
4. Then push existing gyms for reviews.

Changing everything at once means you learn nothing about what worked.
